Staff Categories Explanation

This application categorizes personnel into three operational roles to automate roster balancing and comply with your facility's safety regulations:

Regular Staff

Standard nursing and duty personnel who handle the core roster shifts.

  • Subject to Seniority requirements (e.g. minimum senior staff per shift).
  • Subject to Gender balance rules (e.g. maximum male staff per shift).
  • Individual monthly shift limit caps are applied during roster generation.
Primary Role Category
HA

HA Staff

Auxiliary Hospital Assistants. Names starting with HA or (HA) are automatically designated.

  • Exempt from general Seniority and Gender balance controls.
  • Governed strictly by dedicated HA-specific rules (Min HA per shift, distinct off-after-night recovery).
  • Automatically organized at the bottom of daily printed assignment views.
Exempt / Auxiliary

Counterpart Staff

Relief or trainee staff linked to a primary member to duplicate schedules.

  • Automatically mirrors the exact daily duty assignments of their primary counterpart.
  • Can be active for the entire month or configured for a custom day range.
  • Saves scheduling time and guarantees locked-step pairings.
